# Northern Italy Road Trip

## Trip window

- **Dates:** October 1-7, 2026
- **Travelers:** Peng Dong and Fan Yichen
- **Route:** Milan Malpensa -> Mestre / Venice -> Auronzo -> Tre Cime -> Val di Funes -> Bolzano -> Milan Malpensa
- **Transport:** Centauro rental car, Group F4A (Lynk&Co 01 1.5 or similar)

## Critical checks before departure

1. **Rental pickup time conflict:** The flight lands at Milan Malpensa T1 at 09:50 on October 1, while the rental reminder says pickup at 09:45. Contact Centauro and move pickup to at least 11:00, preferably 11:30, allowing for immigration, baggage and the shuttle to the off-airport branch.
2. **October 3 is the highest-risk driving day:** Tre Cime parking -> Val di Funes is a long mountain transfer after a hike. Start the hike early, leave the parking area by 15:00-16:00, and keep the Funes sunset visit optional.
3. **Mountain lifts and roads are weather-dependent:** Confirm opening status for Seceda / "blade mountain", Resciesa funicular, Alpe di Siusi access rules and Adolf Munkel Trail conditions 24-48 hours before each visit.
4. **October 6 airport buffer:** Leave Bolzano after lunch and target the Centauro return branch around 15:30-16:00. The branch is off-airport in Vizzola Ticino and uses a shuttle to T1; do not plan a late return.
5. **Navigation:** Download offline maps for Venice, the Dolomites and Val di Funes. Mountain roads may have weak signal and seasonal closures.

## Day-by-day reference plan

### October 1, Thursday - Milan arrival, Mestre and Venice

- 09:50 land at Milan Malpensa T1.
- 11:00-11:30 target rental pickup after the booking is corrected. Take the Centauro shuttle from T1 to the Vizzola Ticino branch.
- Drive to the Mestre apartment, leave luggage and have lunch. Keep the first afternoon flexible because the drive and pickup may take longer than expected.
- Afternoon and evening: Venice historic centre, with Piazza San Marco, Rialto and a relaxed canal walk. Return to Mestre for check-in / dinner.

### October 2, Friday - Murano, Burano and Auronzo

- 08:00-10:00 check out and drive / transfer to Venice for the lagoon islands.
- Morning: Murano for glass workshops and canals; continue to Burano for the colourful houses.
- Around noon: lunch, then supermarket stop for the mountain accommodation and hiking days.
- 14:00 depart for Auronzo. This is a long mountain drive; do not add extra Venice sights.
- Around 16:00 check in at Via Professor Carlo Pais 8.
- Evening: Lago di Auronzo and Auronzo town walk if energy and daylight allow.

### October 3, Saturday - Tre Cime and transfer to Val di Funes

- 06:00-06:30 depart Auronzo with breakfast packed.
- 08:00 arrive at Tre Cime parking area, subject to access rules and availability.
- Morning to early afternoon: Cadini / "Ring of the Dolomites" viewpoint as appropriate, then Tre Cime di Lavaredo loop. The exact route depends on weather, trail condition and crowds.
- 15:00-16:00 leave the parking area for Val di Funes.
- Check in at Villa Messner. If the weather is clear and arrival is before sunset, visit the Santa Maddalena / Geisler viewpoint; otherwise rest and move the viewpoint to October 5.

### October 4, Sunday - Seceda / "blade mountain" and Resciesa

- Morning: take the Ortisei lift toward Seceda, locally known for the jagged ridge viewpoint. Confirm the intended lift and ticket online before departure.
- Afternoon: Resciesa / Rasciesa funicular (the requested "棺材缆车"), with a short viewpoint walk if the lift is operating.
- Return to Villa Messner before dark. Keep the afternoon stop optional if the morning mountain visit runs long.

### October 5, Monday - Alpe di Siusi and Adolf Munkel Trail

- Early morning: Alpe di Siusi / Seiser Alm. Check the private-car access restriction and use the permitted cable car or bus where required.
- Afternoon: Adolf Munkel Trail from the Geisler Alm area. Carry water, layers and offline route data; shorten the walk if weather deteriorates.
- Return to Villa Messner for the final night.

### October 6, Tuesday - Bolzano and Milan departure

- Morning: breakfast, pack and check out of Villa Messner.
- 11:00-12:00 lunch in Bolzano, with no extended sightseeing.
- After lunch drive directly to the Centauro branch in Vizzola Ticino.
- Target 15:30-16:00 rental return, then shuttle to Milan Malpensa T1.
- For the 18:55 flight, arrive at the terminal no later than 16:45-17:00.

### October 7, Wednesday - Arrival in Hong Kong

- 17:40 arrive at Hong Kong International Airport T1.
